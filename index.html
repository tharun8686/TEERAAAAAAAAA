TERRA EDGE
About the project

Intelligent Environmental Monitoring & Prediction System

An intelligent environmental monitoring system designed to collect, analyze and interpret real-time environmental data using IoT sensors, embedded systems and Machine Learning. It continuously monitors air quality, smoke, temperature, humidity and other atmospheric conditions — sensor data is captured by the embedded hardware and transmitted to the software platform, where machine-learning models process and analyze it.

Beyond simply displaying sensor readings, the system uses predictive analytics to identify environmental patterns, detect abnormal conditions, estimate risk levels and surface meaningful insight. The goal is to turn raw sensor data into actionable environmental intelligence.

Models

Seven hazard prediction models

Each hazard runs its own independent model, trained on the sensor signals that matter for that specific condition. Every model scores its latest reading into a severity band and raises an alert on its own — nothing has to be triggered by hand.

01 / Inundation
💧
Flood
Tracks river level against soil moisture to catch rapid infiltration and basin inundation before water reaches populated ground.
Water Level Soil Moisture Rainfall 24h
02 / Wildfire
🔥
Forest Fire
Reads smouldering combustion gases alongside rising temperature and falling humidity — the combination that precedes ignition, not heat alone.
Combustion Gas Temp / RH Particulates
03 / Slip Hazard
⛰️
Landslide
Combines soil saturation with micro-angular tilt and vibration, so sudden ground movement registers as an anomaly rather than noise.
Soil Saturation Tilt Rate Vibration
04 / Air Quality
💨
Air Pollution
Projects particulate levels forward from current readings, flagging risk as concentrations approach recognised air-quality thresholds.
PM2.5 / PM10 Gas Array Wind
05 / Heat Risk
☀️
Extreme Heat
Weighs temperature, humidity and solar load together — high temperature on its own isn't always dangerous, but the combination is.
Temperature Humidity Solar Load
06 / Emissions
⚠️
Toxic Emissions
Watches the MQ gas array for a fast-rising reading alongside particulates — the signature of an industrial leak rather than ordinary background drift.
MQ Gas Array Particulates Rate of Change
07 / Water
🌊
Water Quality
Scores how far pH strays from neutral together with turbidity and dissolved solids crossing safe reservoir limits.
pH Turbidity TDS

Alert & risk detection

Four severity levels

MODERATE

WARNING

CRITICAL
Conditions requiring immediate attention have been detected.
